# About me

My name is Elvis Brevi, and I'm a full-stack developer. I cherish family time with my wife and
our two daughters, and one of my greatest passions is expressing my creativity through programming.

Throughout my career, I have worked in almost every technical area of IT: backend, frontend, mobile,
games, desktop, cloud and some DevOps. I have programmed in PHP, C#, Visual Basic, JavaScript,
TypeScript, Java, Python and Rust, and I'm now seeking deep expertise in C#.

I created this personal wiki as my "second brain". I often explore technologies and ideas and
note them down here, as a way to remember and share what I learn.
